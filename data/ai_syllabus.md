# Artificial Intelligence — Starter Course Knowledge

> Starter notes transcribed from the syllabus image supplied with this project. Confirm these topics against the complete official syllabus before relying on them.

## Unit I — Foundations and problem solving
Foundations of Artificial Intelligence: history and evolution; characteristics and types of AI; AI versus machine learning versus deep learning; data science; intelligent systems and AI lifecycle; applications in healthcare, finance, agriculture, manufacturing, robotics, and smart cities. AI problem solving: problem formulation and state-space representation; problem characteristics; search space and solution space; performance measures; overview of AI problem-solving approaches.

## Unit II — Search and knowledge representation
Search techniques: uninformed search (Breadth First Search and Depth First Search), informed search (Best First Search and Hill Climbing), A* search, heuristic functions, comparison and applications of search algorithms. Knowledge representation and reasoning: knowledge representation, semantic networks and frames, production systems and expert systems, propositional logic and first-order predicate logic, reasoning under uncertainty using Bayes' theorem.

## Unit III — Intelligent agents
Intelligent agents and agent architecture; PEAS framework (Performance Measure, Environment, Actuators, Sensors); agent types and environments; rational agents and agent environments; simple reflex agents and model-based agents; goal-based agents and utility-based agents; learning agents; autonomous intelligent systems and multi-agent systems; cooperation and coordination; human-AI collaboration and applications.

## Unit IV — Evolution and modern AI paradigms
Symbolic and statistical AI; overview of machine learning and its types (supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement learning); introduction to deep learning; types and applications of deep learning; foundation models; multimodal foundation models; generative AI; edge AI; tiny AI.

## Unit V — Generative AI and responsible AI
Generative AI concepts and applications; large language models; prompt engineering fundamentals and prompt design techniques; Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG); hallucination in large language models and mitigation strategies. Explainable AI; fairness, bias, and transparency; responsible AI principles; privacy and security; AI governance; ethical and societal implications.

## Unit VI — AI ecosystem and future trends
AI development ecosystem and Python ecosystem; TensorFlow and PyTorch; Hugging Face; Ollama and LangChain; vector databases (conceptual overview); AI development workflow; future trends in AI including Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and sustainable AI.
